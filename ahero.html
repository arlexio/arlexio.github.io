Background

Ahero is a cross-platform focus and productivity app that I designed and built to help students and knowledge workers reduce distractions and focus. The product combines website/app blocking, focus sessions, goal-setting, and accountability into a single system. I worked end-to-end across strategy, UX, visual design, and implementation.

Problem

Difficulty focusing is one of the most common challenges faced by students and knowledge workers today. The same devices used for work and study also provide constant access to the internet, an environment filled with distractions. As a result, many people struggle to stay focused when they are trying to concentrate.

Solution

Ahero addresses distraction by actively blocking selected websites and apps during focus time, based on user-defined block lists, schedules, or time limits. Users can schedule focus sessions and organize the work they want to complete during that time.

The product is built around two core ideas: removing distractions at the moment they matter most, and helping users direct their attention toward meaningful work once those distractions are blocked.

Users can add any website or app that distracts them.

Users can do focus sessions, where items on their blocklist are blocked.

Items in the blocklist are blocked during focus sessions or based on other settings.

Design Challenges

Keeping users in focus mode

A focus session can be ended too easily, which makes it easy for users to get distracted on their devices.

To solve this, I added an option that prevents a session from ending early. The active session also syncs to the phone for a consistent cross-device focus experience.

Blocking is not one-size-fits-all

Blocking preferences are not the same for every user. For example, some users want to block all of Reddit, but still allow one specific subreddit they rely on for work.

To solve this, the block list supports an allow list. Users can block an entire site and whitelist specific URLs, or block only specific subdomains based on their workflow.

Blocking settings can be too easy to disable

Another challenge is that users can quickly turn off blocking settings in moments of low self-control.

To solve this, I added settings locks. Users can lock settings by sending a friend a password, or require typing a long unlock code before changes can be made.

Outcome

Ahero has reached roughly 10,000 signups with around 1,000 monthly active users, and is used by college students and knowledge workers around the world.
